Apple Worm is a Flash era puzzle game where you guide a green worm around small but challenging levels to eat apples. Eat the apple to grow in size but be careful not to trap yourself in places that you can't escape.

In order to cross gaps or make your way past obstacles, you'll need to grow your worm. That's where the apples come into play! With each apple you eat, your worm grows longer. This can make getting to new places easier, but beware! Being large also means you can get stuck and once that happens - it's game over!

There's 30 challenging levels to complete. Can you make it through them all?

How to play Apple Worm?

  • Use WASD or the buttons to move Apple Worm!
